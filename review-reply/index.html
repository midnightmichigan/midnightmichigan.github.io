Your Google reviews are sitting there unanswered — the good ones and the one-star one from last Tuesday. Send them to us. We write the replies, in your voice, and you paste them in. Done in a day.

How it works

  1. 1. You send the reviews. Screenshots or copy-pasted text, whichever is easier, plus your business name and town. That's the whole intake. We never ask for your Google password or account access — the replies are yours to post.
  2. 2. We write every reply. Good reviews get warm, specific thank-yous that mention something real from the review. Bad reviews get calm, non-defensive replies: thank them, acknowledge the specific problem, offer a way to make it right offline. Nothing canned, nothing that sounds like a robot.
  3. 3. You paste them in. The pack arrives by email as one clean document, replies in the same order as your reviews. Most owners clear the whole backlog in about fifteen minutes.
  4. 4. One revision round is included. If a reply doesn't sound like you, tell us how you'd say it and we'll redo it.
