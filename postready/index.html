  1. 1. You send the facts. One short intake: what you sell, your town, your hours, your regulars' favorites, any specials or dates coming up. Ten minutes, once.

What this is — and isn't

PostReady is a writing service. Every post is written from the facts on your intake, and anything your intake does not cover gets flagged for you, never invented. We do not log into your accounts, we do not post for you, and we make no promises about reach, followers, or sales: what you get is a month of real, ready-to-post content and the minutes back every day. Your intake details are used for your pack and nothing else.
