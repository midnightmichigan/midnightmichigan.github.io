🛡️ DeadlineGuardian

Paste a bill, notice, or letter. DeadlineGuardian tells you what it is, how urgent it is, what date and amount matter, and the one next action to take.

100% offlineNo accountNo API keyNothing leaves this page

DeadlineGuardian uses a deterministic, rule-based NLP engine — keyword-signal classification plus date and amount extraction — that runs entirely in your browser. It is not a large language model and it never sends your text anywhere. Always confirm deadlines against the original document.
