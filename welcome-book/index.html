How it works

  1. 1. You send the basics. One short email: address, access and Wi-Fi details, house rules, the quirks guests trip over, check-out steps, and your favorite local spots.
  2. 2. We write the book. Section by section, in a warm host voice, with the local page researched from public sources for your town.

  4. 4. You get the files. PDF for the coffee table and the text version for your message thread, with one revision round included.
