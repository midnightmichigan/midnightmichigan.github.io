What this is — and isn't

Paperwork Concierge is a typing, organizing, and tracking service. We complete forms using only the information you provide, and we flag anything we can't fill from your fact sheet instead of guessing. We are not lawyers, accountants, or tax preparers, and this is not legal or tax advice. We never sign for you, never submit anything in your name without your review, and never keep your documents after your order closes — your files are deleted from our working folder on completion, and your fact sheet is returned to you.
